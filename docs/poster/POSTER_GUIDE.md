## 1. Print Specifications for the Print Shop

When taking the PDF to the print center, provide these exact specifications:
- **Dimensions:** A3 Landscape (420 mm width × 297 mm height)
- **Paper Quality:** 250 GSM or 300 GSM Heavyweight Matte or Satin Photo Paper
- **Color Profile:** CMYK Full Color High-Resolution (300 DPI)
- **Mounting:** Foam board (5mm Sunboard) or pull-up poster stand

## 2. 2-Minute Poster Pitch (Standing by the Booth)

When evaluators stop at your booth:
1. **Greeting & Title (15 seconds):**
   > *"Good morning, respected judges. I am Shaik Mohammed Sameer representing Team P-16 under the guidance of Dr. N. Raghavendra Sai. Our capstone project is CLOUD-223: Application Platform Abstraction Over Heterogeneous Cloud Infrastructure."*
2. **The Problem & Myth (30 seconds):**
   > *"Today, companies assume that if their app compiles on AWS, Azure, and GCP, it's portable. But that is the 'Portability Illusion'. In reality, physical storage controllers and hypervisors cause massive behavioral drift."*
3. **The Solution (30 seconds):**
   > *"We built an abstraction platform that deploys one universal container specification across all three clouds, while our `infraDiffEngine` continuously measures runtime performance using OpenTelemetry."*
4. **The Results (30 seconds):**
   > *"We uncovered 3 major leakage points—storage IOPS dropped 27.1% on Azure compared to AWS, and container cold-start took 61.9% longer. Our measured Behavioral Parity Score is 33.33%, empirically proving our research hypothesis. Furthermore, under controlled chaos pod crashes, our system achieved 100% automated self-healing with an MTTR under 2.8 seconds, all sealed with SHA-256 cryptographic proof."*
5. **Call to Action (15 seconds):**
   > *"We have the live prototype running right here on our laptop. Would you like to see a live multi-cloud deployment or a chaos pod kill test?"*
