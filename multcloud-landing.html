The download and reupload cycle is broken

Moving datasets between clouds using your local machine wastes time and introduces silent file corruption.

The manual workstation loop
  • • Double disk consumption: Downloading massive zip archives fills local drive caches before files even upload.
  • • Sleep interruption: If your Wi Fi drops or your laptop goes to sleep, the browser upload crashes and restarts from zero.
  • • Fractured document layouts: Downloading Google Docs forces them into foreign formats, breaking margins and comment histories.
The direct server pipeline
  • • Zero local space used: Data moves directly from cloud host to cloud host across isolated high capacity backbones.
  • • Autonomous background execution: Shut down your PC, close your browser, or travel; our servers finish the job independently.
  • • Original format retention: Cloud docs, permissions, and media EXIF details remain intact without messy zip extractions.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about cloud to cloud migration, safety, and speeds.

Can I turn off my computer after starting a transfer? ▼

Yes. All file transfers run entirely on isolated server side pipelines. Once you start a transfer task, you can close your browser or turn off your machine, and the transfer continues until finished.

Is my cloud storage account secure with MultCloud? ▼

Yes. Account authorization uses standard OAuth protocols, meaning MultCloud never sees or stores your passwords. All data transit is encrypted using 256 bit AES SSL channels, and files are never permanently saved on our servers.

How much data can I transfer on the free plan? ▼

Every free account receives 5 GB of data transfer traffic every single month at zero charge, with no credit card required to sign up.

Will Google Docs and Sheets convert into broken files? ▼

No. Unlike standard archive downloads that convert Google files into Office files and scramble layouts, MultCloud retains native cloud file structures and formats.

Does MultCloud support scheduled and recurring migrations? ▼

Yes. You can schedule transfers to run daily, weekly, or monthly during off peak hours so your files stay updated on a predictable schedule.

What cloud storage providers can I connect? ▼

MultCloud connects more than forty cloud providers, including Google Drive, Google Workspace, Microsoft OneDrive, SharePoint, Dropbox, iCloud Photos, Box, MEGA, Amazon S3, and standard FTP servers.

Can I transfer only specific file formats? ▼

Yes. The built in file filter allows you to include or exclude specific extensions such as video or image files, helping you migrate exactly what you need.

What happens if a network interruption occurs? ▼

Because migrations execute server to server rather than through your local internet, local network drops do not affect the task. The system automatically retries interrupted blocks to guarantee full file integrity.
